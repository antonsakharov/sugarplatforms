# Delivery Plan

Build the real limited diagnostic first, with the sample assessment as a parallel demonstration path.

## Delivery sequence
1. Deployable product shell and assessment setup.
2. Guided upload and validation.
3. Parsing and source-addressable evidence.
4. Structured extraction and review.
5. Evidence-backed diagnostics and finding review.
6. Entity/ID visualization.
7. Maturity, recommendations, 90-day plan, executive report, and PDF export.

## Release gate
A phase is complete only when the user-visible workflow works, validation passes, limits are enforced, evidence requirements are preserved, documentation is current, and the sample remains functional.
